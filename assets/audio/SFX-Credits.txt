Sources were trimmed, filtered, resampled to mono 44.1 kHz, gain-adjusted, and in some cases rate-shifted. Specific mappings and processing are recorded in docs/sfx-pass-measurements.json in the source repository. Damped chimes and filtered elemental textures were generated for Astra. Music and ambient loops were not edited.
Generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects (Justin's paid plan), 2026-10-16: the sigil-*, spell-* cues and the replacements listed in approved.json. sigil-use: synthesized by Claude (numpy + ffmpeg).
boss-grukk music: made by Justin with ElevenLabs Music ('Beneath the Torchlit Arch'), 2026-10-16; amb-fire, goblin-attack, spell-shadowbolt-cast: ElevenLabs Sound Effects.
