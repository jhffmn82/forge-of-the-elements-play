## Gameplay and balance

- Monsters notice you less from afar. Within 3 tiles the chance is as before; at 4-5 tiles it is 70% of that, at 6-7 tiles 30%, and beyond 7 they do not notice you by sight at all (a wandering monster used to spot you at full chance from 9 tiles).
- A monster that wakes goes to where it noticed you and looks around, instead of knowing where you are from then on. One that heard you through a wall used to follow you across the whole floor, which is where the long trains of monsters came from.
- You spot traps in passing twice as often: 8% plus 3% per Agility above 10 (was 4% plus 2%). The Scoundrel's bonus is unchanged.
- Tithing at another god's shrine now blesses you for 250 turns (was 40).
- Mother Murk: your summons (servant, Shades, Shadow Swarm, Living Flame, your Shadow) have +5% HP and +5% damage per rank, and from rank 3 they move 10% faster. Life Drain now gives each hit you or your summons land a 10% chance to heal you 1 HP per rank, instead of healing on a kill. The Lich is unchanged.
- Each run's four shrine gods are now dealt from the gods who will take your character, so every shrine you find offers a god you can swear to. A Mage no longer has to restart a run hoping for a god that accepts them.
- Gods now refuse some classes: Mages cannot follow Chad, Grumbok or Sir Reginald; Scoundrels cannot follow Sir Reginald or Vellum; Fighters cannot follow Vellum or Sylla. Clerics and Tourists may follow any god that accepts their race. If you already follow one of these gods, nothing changes.
- Sound effects are quieter overall. Every effect louder than the weapon combat sounds (swings, hits, parries, blocks) is brought down to their level; combat sounds, crate and pot breaks and the bag pickup are unchanged, and music is untouched.
- On top of that, a goblin's death, casting Magic Missile, the essence chime, the level-up sting and the identify chime ("You now know your...") are at half that level, and walking through grass is quieter again.
