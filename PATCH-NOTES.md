# Forge of the Elements: Beta 1.3.5 hotfix

Changes since Beta 1.3.4.

## Graphics and presentation

## Interface

- Everything you activate from the hotbar is now simply called an ability in game text: the hotbar, hover cards, the Faith tab, learning messages, click abilities, the Cleric's god ability and the Holy Symbol's God ability strength.

## Gameplay and balance

- A Shadow weapon enchant now deals +2 damage to Corrupted targets (it was +1 to Hollowed targets). The enchant can Corrupt the target itself.
- Rot now stops all HP regeneration, including a Light armor enchant, Grumbok's Thick Hide, a Ring of Mending and the Regeneration food buff, and halves every other heal: hearts, potions, healing abilities and life-draining effects.
- Grumbok's Thick Hide now gives +4% magic and elemental resistance per rank (was 8%); its +20% HP regeneration per rank is unchanged.
- Grumbok's Wizard Hunter now also wakes when a ranged attack hurts you.
- Grumbok's rank-5 boon Spellbreaker is replaced by Warrior's Discipline: your abilities recharge 1 turn faster per rank (never under 1 turn), and each ability you use grants +2 damage for 6 turns, stacking once per rank.
- Rampage now gives +20% melee damage and +10% melee attack speed, plus 4% and 2% per rank (40% and 20% at rank 5).
- Trollblood now heals 20% of max HP plus 5% per rank (45% at rank 5).
- Sap now has an 8-turn cooldown, on top of its mana cost.
- An Air weapon enchant now has a 10% chance to strike twice even without Air affinity, plus 5% per Air point (it was 5% per point with a 5% floor).
- A Light weapon enchant is now Hallowed Edge: each hit with the weapon, and each spell it carries, heals you 1 HP per Light point (at least 1). It replaces the enchant's crit chance bonus. Light enchants on other gear are unchanged.
